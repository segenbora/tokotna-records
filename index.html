Tokota Breeding Records v?

Slots, breedings and genos, read from the comments on TokoTime’s DeviantArt request journals. Look up a person or a tokota.

Slots they granted or received, plus their breedings and any genos still owed.

2015 male slots are marked as VOID as these were written when males had unlimited slots.

Slots or genos on broken journals are marked as BROKEN and cannot be used.

Some slot permissions are marked as “Unclear”. This is in cases where the script can’t be certain whether the slot was used or not, please use your own judgement.
